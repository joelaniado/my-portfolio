01

Do Discounts Affect Customer Ratings?

An end-to-end e-commerce analysis examining whether discounted purchases are associated with different customer ratings. I built the analytical dataset from relational Olist data using SQL and DuckDB, estimated the historical relationship with fixed-effects regression, and designed a randomized A/B test to answer the causal question prospectively.
