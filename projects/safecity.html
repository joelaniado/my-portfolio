SafeCity

CategoryData Visualization, Data Wrangling, Statistical Analysis
ToolsHTML, CSS, D3.js, Google Maps API
RoleTeam project
Problem Approach Result

Problem

Prospective renters and homebuyers in L.A. have no quick way to compare neighborhood safety across multiple crime categories in one place — most crime data is scattered across raw reports rather than presented visually.

Approach

Built, as a team, an interactive visualization combining 10 crime categories with a Google Maps-based interface using D3.js, letting users rank and explore neighborhoods visually rather than digging through spreadsheets.

Result

A working interactive tool, documented in a team poster and full paper, that turns raw crime data into a quick visual safety comparison across neighborhoods.
